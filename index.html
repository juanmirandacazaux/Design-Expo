About The Expo

PropConnect Expo 2025 is a global gathering of architects, developers, designers, and creative technologists exploring the intersection between physical and digital environments.

Our Mission

To bridge creativity and technology through collaboration, innovation, and hands-on experiences that inspire the next generation of design leaders.

Who Attends

Architects, interior designers, urban planners, UX designers, engineers, and forward-thinking brands shaping tomorrow’s spaces.

Why It Matters

The future of design lies in connected systems. PropConnect creates meaningful conversations around sustainable and intelligent environments.
